"01 — THE IDEA"

Choosing subjects in Grade 10 can feel like making a decision about a future that hasn't been fully discovered yet.

Learner_Career uses data-driven intelligence to help make that connection clearer.

It analyses a learner's interests, strengths and preferences to identify subject pathways that may align with different career fields.

«Not a decision-maker. A direction-finder.»

About

Learner_Career is a data-driven intelligence designed to help Grade 10 learners explore subject choices that align with their interests, strengths and potential career fields.

Rather than simply recommending subjects, the system explores the relationship between:

«What a learner enjoys → What they choose → What they can study → Where those choices can lead.»

The goal is to provide direction without deciding the learner's future for them.

---

THink.DWanaY

THink.DWanaY is a major part of the thinking foundation behind Learner_Career.

It provides a structured approach to transforming a real-world question into an intelligent data-driven solution:

OBSERVE → THINK → QUESTION → CONNECT → ANALYSE → DIRECT

This framework encourages the system to look beyond the final recommendation and consider how the problem should be understood before the intelligence is built.

«Intelligence begins with how we think about the problem.»
